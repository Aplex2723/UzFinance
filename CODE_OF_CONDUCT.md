# Code of conduct

We want UzFinance to be a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity or nationality.

**Be kind and constructive.** Assume good faith, critique code and ideas rather than people, and welcome newcomers' questions.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualised language or attention, doxxing (including sharing anyone's financial data), spam, or trolling.

**Maintainers** may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and PRs that violate this code, and may restrict participation of those who do so repeatedly.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the profile at <https://github.com/Aplex2723>. Reports are handled confidentially.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org>).
